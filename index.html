HOW TO NAVIGATE

❖

A QUICK GUIDE TO USING COLLEGE NAVIGATOR

1

EXPLORE THE MAP

Move around the CSE Block Level 1 map freely using the on-screen controls. Explore the campus and familiarize yourself with the area.

2

FIND A DESTINATION

Open Destination Search to browse rooms and facilities. Use the search bar or category filters to find your destination.

3

SELECT A DESTINATION

Tap on a destination to select it. The selected destination will be saved and shown as your target on the map.

4

NAVIGATE MANUALLY

Move your character towards the destination using the on-screen controls. Follow the map and landmarks to reach your destination.

5

REACH YOUR DESTINATION

Arrive at the destination and explore the facility. You have successfully navigated the campus!

TIP

Take your time to explore! College Navigator helps you learn the campus and find your way around easily.
